# RobotExpressive

Model by Tomás Laulhé (Quaternius), licensed CC0 1.0 Universal.
Modifications by Don McCurdy: facial expression morph targets, FBX2GLTF conversion,
duplicate material removal and reduced metalness.

Downloaded 2026-09-22 from the official Three.js example repository:
https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/dev/examples/models/gltf/RobotExpressive
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mrdoob/three.js/dev/examples/models/gltf/RobotExpressive/RobotExpressive.glb
License notice: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/dev/examples/models/gltf/RobotExpressive/README.md
License text: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Creator: https://quaternius.com/

The GLB is unchanged. Resonance scales it at runtime to 1.75 m in its reference pose.
metadata.json records the rigid-mesh vertical bounds used for normalization; animations
naturally change the silhouette. Idle, Walking and Running are supplied by the asset.
